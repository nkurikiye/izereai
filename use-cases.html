Managing Authorities

Managing authorities are directly accountable for programme absorption rates, milestone compliance, and audit readiness. They operate under intense political and EC scrutiny — and their monitoring tools have not kept pace with programme complexity.

⚠ Detection too late
Delays and cost overruns are surfaced at programme reviews, not weeks before they materialise.
⚠ Fragmented delivery data
Performance data is spread across dozens of beneficiaries and reporting systems with no unified view.
⚠ Audit pressure
EC and national audit institutions require traceable, evidence-based oversight — not self-reported dashboards.
How Izere Helps
Detect execution risks 2–3 months before programme reviews
Provide EC auditors with traceable, XAI-explainable oversight evidence
Reduce repetitive due diligence by reusing delivery intelligence across cycles
Improve absorption rates by identifying bottlenecks while corrective action is still possible

EIB · KfW · EBRD · SNCI · National Development Banks

DFIs & Public Banks

Development finance institutions co-finance EU infrastructure and green investments. Execution reliability directly affects bankability, disbursement schedules, and portfolio risk — but DFIs currently lack visibility into delivery performance between reporting periods.

⚠ Subcontractor opacity
The subcontractor layer is effectively invisible. Prime contractors are visible in TED; who actually delivers is not.
⚠ Selection intelligence gap
Due diligence relies on track records that are qualitative, non-standardised, and not portable across programmes.
⚠ Post-commitment blind spots
Risk monitoring ends at contract signature. Delivery performance during implementation is opaque.
How Izere Helps
Structure delivery track records for applicants and their subcontractor chains
Generate OLAF-aligned fraud risk indicators for pre-award due diligence
Monitor post-award execution in real time across DFI portfolio
Produce contestable, human-overseen intelligence briefings — not automated exclusion recommendations

Infrastructure Developers & EPCs

Large infrastructure developers and EPCs operate distributed delivery networks reliant on SMEs and subcontractors. Early visibility into subcontractor capacity and coordination risk is a commercial imperative — and increasingly a condition of EU-funded contract compliance.

⚠ SME capacity opacity
SME and subcontractor execution capacity is hard to assess at procurement. Failures compound across the delivery chain.
⚠ Change event management
Design revisions, environmental permits, and coordination failures are poorly tracked across multi-actor programmes.
⚠ Compliance documentation burden
EU co-funded contracts require extensive performance documentation that is costly to produce manually.
How Izere Helps
Assess subcontractor delivery capacity before contract award
Monitor multi-tier delivery chains in real time
Automate milestone and change-event documentation for EU compliance
Generate early warnings on coordination failures within the delivery network

Technical Assistance Firms

Technical assistance firms and consortium leads are responsible for programme design, implementation support, and monitoring. They are increasingly under pressure to demonstrate delivery effectiveness — not just compliance.

⚠ Delivery effectiveness vs compliance
EC and managing authorities are moving toward outcome-based accountability — TA firms must follow.
⚠ Data non-portability
Insights from one programme cannot be reused in the next. Knowledge is locked in static reports.
⚠ Competitive differentiation
Differentiating TA proposals on delivery intelligence capability is a growing competitive requirement.
How Izere Helps
Embed Izere as a delivery-intelligence work package in EU consortium proposals
Demonstrate programme effectiveness with XAI-evidenced outcomes
Reuse delivery intelligence across programme cycles
Win competitive proposals by offering continuous intelligence rather than periodic reports
