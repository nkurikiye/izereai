Start small. Test what is useful.

The pilot is adapted to your governance, data access and decision process. The aim is validation—not a large technology deployment.

01 / SCOPE

Define the question.

Select the project, users and decision to support. Agree the evidence that can be reviewed and what success means.

02 / REVIEW

Connect the evidence.

Structure the selected records, map dependencies and examine candidate warning signs with the delivery team.

03 / EVALUATE

Measure usefulness.

Compare findings with expert judgement and assess evidence quality, warning value and time saved.

What the pilot produces.

A practical evidence package your team can review—not a black-box score.

DELIVERABLE 01

Project evidence map.

Selected sources linked to the contracts, milestones, actors and dependencies they describe.

DELIVERABLE 02

Priority warning report.

A small set of reviewable findings with drivers, provenance and recommended checks.

DELIVERABLE 03

Team review.

A structured session to test findings against operational knowledge and document decisions.

DELIVERABLE 04

Validation note.

An honest assessment of evidence gaps, usefulness and whether a broader deployment is justified.
