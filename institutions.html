The problem

The problem

Young adults want to invest.
Most were never taught how.

They save, follow financial content and hear that starting early matters. But the first investment journey often begins with products, disclosures and questionnaires—before understanding and confidence have been built.

01

“I want to get started.”

But the first step feels designed for experts.

Terms such as ETF, volatility and diversification appear before the person has had a chance to see what they mean in practice.

02

“I have some savings.”

But investing still feels unfamiliar.

The person wants their money to grow, but choosing an investment feels difficult—and making a mistake feels expensive.

03

“We already offer investment products.”

But confidence does not come from a product page.

Financial institutions have the products and trusted customer relationships, yet product pages alone rarely create understanding or confidence.

People need somewhere to explore, ask simple questions and learn by doing—before risking real money or entering a regulated investment journey.

WHY IZERE EXISTSWhen people understand what they are doing, taking the next step feels natural.

Why it matters

A personal challenge.
A European opportunity.

When more people understand how investing works, household savings can support their long-term goals—and help finance the businesses and ideas Europe needs.

Izere is not presented as a compliance substitute. It provides an education and evidence layer that can sit upstream of institution-owned suitability, advice and execution.

The Izere activation layer

Educate clearly.
Build confidence. Measure readiness.

Three connected capabilities create a more human first step into investing and a measurable path into the institution’s journey.

01 · ADAPTIVE EDUCATION

Explain concepts in language people understand.

AI adapts language, examples and difficulty within an institution-approved curriculum—while Bakame helps learners understand saving, investing and risk without recommending products.

02 · SAFE SIMULATION

Let people practise before money is at risk.

Interactive scenarios make compounding, inflation, diversification and uncertainty visible. Learners can test assumptions and see trade-offs without committing real money.

03 · MEASURABLE HANDOFF

Turn understanding into a qualified next step.

Completion, comprehension and expressed interest become visible before the customer continues into the institution’s own product, suitability and execution journey.

A human face for adaptive learning

Meet Bakame, the AI coach who makes the first step feel possible.

Bakame explains approved concepts, invites reflection and supports practice in plain language. He never selects a product, promises a return or replaces the institution’s advice and suitability journey.
