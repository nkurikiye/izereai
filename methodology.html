Methodology

If your auditor cannot read it, do not deploy it.

Izere is being designed so every warning can be traced from its source evidence to the feature values, model output and human decision. Production claims will require prospective pilot validation.

01 · Problem02 · Evidence03 · Model04 · Explanation05 · Evaluation06 · Limits

01 / PROBLEM

Predict early enough to support a decision.

The intended outcome is not a generic risk label. It is a reviewable warning about a delivery condition—such as procurement concentration, design immaturity or an unresolved dependency—before the affected milestone is formally missed.

The target, decision window and responsible user must be defined before modelling begins.

02 / EVIDENCE

Keep project facts connected to their provenance.

Izere’s proposed project model links programmes, projects, contracts, organisations, milestones, changes and funding events. Each fact retains its source, document date, extraction date and confidence.

Evidence classExamplesControl
Project profileSector, budget, planned duration, contract typeFixed at the relevant prediction date
Execution evidenceMilestone changes, procurement events, permitsTimestamped and linked to source
ContextInstitutional and fiscal conditionsVersioned external data

03 / MODEL

Use the simplest model that answers the question.

Baseline rules and conventional models should be tested before graph-based methods. A graph model becomes relevant when relationships—such as shared contractors, dependent milestones or fragmented responsibilities—carry information that flat tables lose.

Proposed production record

  • Model class and version
  • Training and validation windows
  • Feature definitions and cut-off dates
  • Known data gaps and performance by project type

04 / EXPLANATION

Ship the evidence chain with the score.

Every warning should show the input values that moved, their contribution, the affected project objects and the source evidence. Explanations must be readable by a delivery officer and reproducible by an auditor.

Human reviewers must be able to challenge the source, correct a fact and record why they accepted or rejected the warning.

05 / EVALUATION

Measure usefulness, not only accuracy.

Pilots should evaluate whether Izere produces earlier, better-supported decisions—not merely whether a numerical score looks plausible.

MeasureQuestion
Evidence qualityAre the sources complete, timely and correctly linked?
Warning qualityDo experts judge the finding relevant and sufficiently early?
Decision valueDoes the warning change a review, escalation or mitigation action?
Time savedDoes the team reach a documented conclusion faster?

06 / LIMITS

State clearly what has not been proven.

The public demo uses illustrative rules and retrospective scenarios. It is not a deployed prediction service. Rail Baltica is not an Izere customer, and the case study does not establish that Izere would have predicted or prevented the outcome.

Prospective pilots are required to test model performance, operational usefulness, governance and conformity requirements before production deployment.
