The solution

From scattered evidence to clearer decisions.

Evidence in. Reviewable action out.

Izere organises project information around the programme, actors, contracts, milestones and dependencies it describes. Each warning remains linked to its source.

01 / ASSESS

Establish readiness.

Review design maturity, permits, procurement, funding and governance before the next commitment.

02 / CONNECT

Follow dependencies.

Link a change or warning to the milestones, contracts and responsible actors it can affect.

03 / ACT

Make the next move.

Prioritise what needs review, assign responsibility and keep the decision connected to its evidence.

One issue. Several consequences.

Designed for institutional review.

The product supports judgement; it does not replace it. Every flag should be explainable, contestable and usable in an audit conversation.

01 / CONTEXT

A connected project model.

Programmes, projects, contracts, actors, milestones and changes share a consistent vocabulary.

02 / PROVENANCE

Sources stay attached.

Dates, documents and input values travel with the finding instead of disappearing into a black box.

03 / EXPLANATION

Drivers are visible.

Teams can see what changed, why the warning moved and which dependency requires attention.

04 / HUMAN REVIEW

People decide.

Officers investigate the evidence, document their judgement and choose the appropriate intervention.
