Request Information

No per-user pricing

Contracts are structured around programme and portfolio usage, not individual user seats. Price reflects institutional value — risk reduction, earlier detection, improved absorption.

EU pilot entry points

Initial deployments can be embedded in EU-funded programmes and consortia, with costs funded through EU or donor programme budgets. Pilots convert to annual contracts.

Multi-year contracts welcome

Multi-year framework agreements reduce onboarding costs and allow delivery intelligence to compound over successive programme cycles. Preferred by managing authorities and DFIs.

Why This Model

Pricing that reflects what institutions actually buy

🏛

Institutional procurement patterns

EU public authorities and DFIs procure through annual contracts, framework agreements, and programme-level line items — not monthly SaaS subscriptions. Our structure aligns with how institutional budgets actually work.

📊

Value = risk reduction, not features

A single avoided cost overrun on a €200M programme can return 10–50× the cost of an Izere contract. We price against the risk we eliminate, not the number of dashboards we provide.

🔄

Compounding intelligence value

Delivery intelligence compounds over time. Each programme cycle builds on the last. Multi-year contracts reflect this — and allow us to significantly reduce per-programme costs as the dataset deepens.

🛡

No freemium, no lock-in anxiety

We don't offer free tiers that create dependency. Every engagement starts with a scoped, paid deliverable. This builds trust and removes the anxiety of hidden upgrade pressure.
