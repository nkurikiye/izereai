The execution gap

Europe can commit capital at scale.
It cannot systematically convert it into delivery.

EU-funded programmes depend on fragmented ecosystems of SMEs, subcontractors, utilities, and regional authorities. Delivery-relevant data is dispersed, qualitative, and not reusable across programmes.
35% of EU public-investment projects experience significant delays, cost overruns, or performance shortfalls. Source · ECA, OECD
€2.3T in EU public procurement annually — execution risk is the primary bottleneck, not capital. Source · DG GROW
7% of project value spent on repetitive due diligence, monitoring, and ex-post audits — with poor early visibility. Source · institutional benchmarks
11% of the 2021–2027 Cohesion Policy envelope implemented by August 2025 — worst start on record. Source · DG REGIO

Execution risks are detected too late. By the time traditional audits surface a problem, remediation is expensive or impossible. Consulting firms provide episodic insights — not continuous intelligence.

  1. 01No real-time visibility into delivery capacity across complex multi-actor chains.
  2. 02Performance data is administrative, siloed, and non-reusable across programmes.
  3. 03Audit firms provide episodic insights — not the continuous, programme-level layer needed.
  4. 04No early-warning intelligence to identify risk early enough to intervene.

The platform

One execution data model.
Four intelligence layers.

Architecturally, Izere sits beside existing systems and reads what they already produce. Every layer is independently auditable, every output independently contestable.
L1

Connectors & ingestion

Native integrations with EU public-data systems (TED, PPDS, Arachne, ESPD), national programme-management platforms, and procurement databases. API-first; nothing proprietary required.

L2

Execution data model

Programme, project, contract, actor, and signal entities — unified into a portable schema that survives across instruments, geographies, and Member States.

L3

Explainable risk engine

Graph neural networks for actor-relationship modelling. SHAP-based explanations on every score. Federated learning so insights compound without data ever leaving an institution's perimeter.

L4

Decision surface

Programme dashboards, risk feeds, audit packs, and structured intervention playbooks — designed for managing authorities and DFI investment officers, not data scientists.
