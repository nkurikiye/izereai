About Alain

Across capital,
enterprise and impact.

Alain Nkurikiye is a Luxembourg-based entrepreneur, investor and development-finance specialist working across Africa and Europe.

His experience spans investment readiness, SME finance, venture building and the design and delivery of innovation programmes. He has worked with entrepreneurs, financial institutions and development organisations to translate capital into businesses, jobs and long-term economic opportunity.

Across that work, one pattern kept returning: people are interested in building wealth, but the language, interfaces and pathways into investing remain unnecessarily difficult. Financial institutions have trusted products and established customer relationships, yet often lack an engaging bridge between curiosity and informed action.

Alain founded Izere to build that bridge—combining adaptive education, scenario-based learning and responsible institutional handoffs so that more people can understand money before making financial decisions.

Founder conviction

“Democratising investment starts before the first transaction. It starts when someone understands what their money can do—and what risks they are taking.”
