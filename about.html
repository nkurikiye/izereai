The ownership gap

Ownership should not require insider knowledge.

More people can become investors and entrepreneurs when complex financial ideas are made understandable, practical and safe to explore.

Why Izere exists

Aspiration is widespread.
The pathway is not.

People are encouraged to save, invest and build businesses. Yet the knowledge required to become an owner is still presented through specialist language, high-stakes decisions and systems designed for people who already understand them.

01

Saving is familiar. Ownership is not.

Many people know how to put money aside but have never had a safe place to see how investing, time and risk interact.

02

Products arrive before understanding.

Investment journeys often begin with disclosures and questionnaires before confidence and comprehension have been built.

03

Entrepreneurship hides its mechanics.

Ideas are celebrated, while equity, dilution, capital and founder wealth remain difficult to understand before the stakes become real.

Izere closes the understanding gap between financial aspiration and informed action.

The Izere method

From earning
to owning.

Income can create stability. Ownership creates participation in long-term value. Izere helps people understand the paths from saving to investing—and from an idea to an enterprise they can own.

The trusted learning interface

Meet Bakame.
A cultural bridge to complex ideas.

Inspired by the hare as a symbol of intelligence, adaptability and resourcefulness, Bakame is Izere’s AI learning coach. He turns financial and entrepreneurial concepts into plain-language explanations, reflective questions and safe simulations.

Bakame supports understanding. He does not recommend products, promise returns or replace professional advice.

The practitioner behind Izere

Built across capital,
enterprise and execution.

Alain Nkurikiye is a Luxembourg-based entrepreneur, investor and development-finance specialist.

His experience spans investment readiness, SME finance, venture building and the design and delivery of innovation programmes. He has worked with entrepreneurs, financial institutions and development organisations to connect capital with businesses, jobs and long-term economic opportunity.

One pattern kept returning: ambition is widely distributed, but the knowledge, confidence and pathways required to become an owner are not. Alain founded Izere to make those pathways visible, understandable and practical.
