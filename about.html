Our purpose

Help teams act
while there is still time.

Project information is often spread across reports, procurement records, schedules and correspondence. Izere’s ambition is to bring that evidence together so teams can investigate warning signs and make informed interventions.

Evidence first.

A useful warning should lead back to the information behind it. Findings need context, source references and a clear explanation.

People in control.

Project teams bring the judgement. AI-assisted evidence review is intended to support their work, with findings checked by people.
