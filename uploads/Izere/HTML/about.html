OUR MISSION

Creating Economic Opportunity Through Digital Trust

Every day, 400 million African workers wake up and go to work. They drive taxis, build homes, sell goods, provide services. They are skilled, reliable, and essential to their communities.

Yet to banks, lenders, and formal employers, they simply don't exist. No credit history. No verifiable identity. No proof of reliability. They are digitally invisible.

Izere changes this. We use voice biometrics and AI to create verified digital identities that informal workers can carry with them throughout their lives - building reputation, accessing credit, and unlocking economic mobility.

OUR STORY

Why This Matters

Meet Jean. Every morning at 5 AM, he wakes up in Kigali, checks his motorcycle, and begins another day as a moto taxi driver. He's been doing this for eight years. He knows every street, every shortcut. His regular customers trust him completely. He's never missed a payment to his cooperative. He's reliable, skilled, and essential to his community.

But to the bank downtown, Jean doesn't exist.

"I wanted to buy a better motorcycle—one that uses less fuel, breaks down less. I needed $800. The bank asked for employment letters, bank statements, a credit score. I had none of those. I have eight years of customer reviews in my head. Eight years of on-time payments. Eight years of building a reputation. But none of it counted. To them, I was invisible."

— Jean, composite from 50+ interviews with informal workers

The Numbers Behind the Stories

Jean isn't alone. There are 400 million workers just like him across Sub-Saharan Africa. Every day, they:

  • Wake up at dawn and work 12-hour days building homes, driving taxis, selling goods, providing services that keep cities running
  • Generate $10 trillion in annual economic activity—more than the GDP of Germany and France combined
  • Support families, pay school fees, invest in their communities—all without a single credit score
  • Build reputations through word-of-mouth, community endorsements, years of consistent work

And yet, when they walk into a bank, they're told the same thing: "We cannot verify you."

The System is Broken—For Everyone

This isn't just a problem for workers. Banks and microfinance institutions are losing billions because they cannot distinguish between reliable borrowers and risky ones.

The Bank's Dilemma:

  • ❌
    Reject 70% of applicants? You lose a massive market opportunity. That's $100 billion in lending potential left on the table.
  • ❌
    Lend blindly? Default rates hit 15-30%, destroying profitability and forcing higher interest rates that hurt everyone.
  • ❌
    Manual verification? Send field officers to verify each applicant at $50-150 per visit. A $200 loan becomes unprofitable.

So banks do what any rational institution would do: they say no. They reject Jean. They reject the market vendor who's never missed a rent payment. They reject the construction worker who's built half the new buildings in town.

The result? Workers remain trapped in poverty despite years of hard work. Banks miss out on billions in sustainable lending. Economies grow slower than they should.

"This isn't a lack of credit demand. It's not a lack of creditworthy borrowers. It's a lack of trust infrastructure—a way to make reputation portable, verifiable, and actionable."

The Invisible Tax of Being Invisible

When you can't be verified, everything costs more. You pay higher interest to informal lenders. You can't bulk-buy materials to save money. You can't invest in better equipment that would make you more efficient. You can't smooth income across bad months.

Research shows that informal workers pay an "invisibility tax" of 20-40% annually—money that could go to school fees, healthcare, or business growth, lost simply because the financial system can't see them.

What $800 Could Mean

For Jean, that $800 loan for a better motorcycle could:

What We're Building

Izere creates portable, verified digital identities for informal workers using just their voice. No documents required. No literacy barriers. No app downloads.

Here's how it works for Jean:

  1. 1 One-Time Setup (30 seconds): Jean calls a WhatsApp number and records a 30-second voice sample. Our AI creates a unique voiceprint—like a fingerprint, but using his voice.
  2. 2 Build Trust Score: As Jean works, our AI tracks behavioral signals. Does he show up on time? Do customers re-hire him? Does he maintain his cooperative payments? All of this builds a Trust Score (0-100).
  3. 3 Apply for Loan: When Jean needs that motorcycle loan, the bank queries our API. In 2 seconds, they get: verified identity + trust score (say, 87/100) + full risk assessment.
  4. 4 Get the Loan: Bank lends confidently because they have actual data. Jean gets his motorcycle. His score improves as he repays. Next loan is easier. Upward mobility unlocked.

Cost to verify Jean manually: $50-150
Cost with Izere: $0.50-1.00
Time to verify manually: 3-7 days
Time with Izere: 2 seconds

The Path Forward

Phase 1: Moto Taxi Drivers
