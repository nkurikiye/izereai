THE PROBLEM

African Banks Cannot Verify Informal Workers

85% of Sub-Saharan workforce is informal. No credit history, no formal records, no way to assess creditworthiness. Banks lose billions while millions remain unbanked.

🏦

For Banks & MFIs

Manual verification costs $50-150 per applicant. Traditional credit bureaus cover less than 10% of the population. Result: 70% rejection rate and massive opportunity loss.

Default rates: 15-30% on informal loans
👷

For Informal Workers

No digital identity means no proof of reliability. Skilled workers remain trapped in cash-only, word-of-mouth economy with no path to financial inclusion or upward mobility.

400M workers digitally invisible
📱

For Gig Platforms

Cannot verify drivers, delivery workers, or domestic staff at scale. Document fraud is rampant. Trial-and-error hiring wastes money and creates safety risks.

40% of submitted IDs are fake or borrowed
THE SOLUTION

Voice Biometrics + AI Trust Scoring

If you can speak, you can be verified. We use voice-first technology to create verified digital identities for workers without documents, literacy requirements, or app downloads.

1

🎤 Voice Biometric Registration

Worker calls WhatsApp number, records 30-second voice sample. AI creates unique voiceprint that cannot be faked or borrowed. Works in any language, requires no literacy.

Tech: Azure Voice API (99.8% accuracy) + liveness detection
2

📊 Behavioral Trust Scoring

AI analyzes 50+ signals: payment consistency, peer endorsements from community networks, task completion rates, geographic patterns, transaction history. Generates Trust Score (0-100) updated in real-time.

Output: Risk assessment + reliability index + fraud detection
3

🔗 API Access for Institutions

Bank queries worker by phone number → instant identity verification + trust score + comprehensive risk assessment. Reduces verification cost from $50 to under $1. Integration takes 2 weeks.

Integration: REST API, webhook alerts, real-time dashboard
