The Problem

Europe can commit capital at scale. It cannot systematically convert it into delivery.

EU-funded programmes depend on fragmented ecosystems of SMEs, subcontractors, utilities, and regional authorities. Delivery-relevant data is dispersed across siloed systems, largely qualitative, and not reusable.

Execution risks are detected too late. By the time traditional audits surface a problem, remediation is expensive or impossible.

  • No real-time visibility into delivery capacity across complex multi-actor chains
  • Performance data is administrative, siloed, and non-reusable across programmes
  • Consulting and audit firms provide episodic insights — not continuous intelligence
  • No programme-level layer that identifies risk early enough to intervene
