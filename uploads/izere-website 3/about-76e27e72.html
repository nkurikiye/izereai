About Izere

Built at the intersection of institutional experience and explainable AI.

Izere was founded on a simple observation: Europe's execution gap is not a technology problem, a funding problem, or a regulatory problem. It is an intelligence problem.

The data to detect delivery risk earlier already exists within every programme. It is fragmented, qualitative, and non-reusable. Izere exists to change that.

The founder-market fit that matters

Alain Nkurikiye brings 18 years of direct experience across EU-funded programmes, multilateral development finance, and public-sector infrastructure delivery. He has worked with DFIs, EU institutions, and international agencies — advising on programme design, bankability assessment, and execution-risk management across instruments including the RRF, Cohesion Policy, ESF+, and Green Deal.

This background is not incidental to Izere. It is the reason the platform exists. The delivery-intelligence gap Izere addresses is one Alain observed first-hand, across multiple programmes and geographies. The product is built by someone who has sat on both sides of the monitoring table.

Izere's long-term vision — DELIX, Delivery Infrastructure for Public Investment Capital — is to become the intelligence backbone for how Europe's public investment capital is deployed, monitored, and improved over time.

What We Believe

Principles that shape every product decision

01

Infrastructure framing over tool framing

Izere is not a dashboard. It is decision infrastructure for public investment capital. That framing changes what we build, how we price, and who we sell to.

02

Explainability is non-negotiable

Every risk score must be contestable. Every output must include an evidence chain. We build for institutions that will be audited — so our platform must be audit-ready.

03

Non-dilutive first

EU grants and R&D support before equity. Capital-efficient growth over venture-scale moonshots. We build for the long term.

04

Category creation over competition

We operate in white space. The right positioning is not "better than X" — it is "the only platform that does Y". We build for the gap, not the market.
