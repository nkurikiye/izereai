The Platform

Execution intelligence.
Explainable by design.

Izere is a modular, cloud-native delivery-intelligence platform built for EU public-sector environments. It analyses delivery data that already exists within your programmes and transforms it into structured, auditable early-warning insights.

Architecture

Three-layer delivery intelligence

Izere is not a replacement for your existing systems. It is an intelligence layer that sits across them — ingesting, structuring, and analysing the execution data already being generated.

Layer 1

Data Ingestion & Structuring

Secure APIs ingest delivery-relevant information from procurement systems, programme-management tools, milestone reports, disbursement records, and coordination signals. Data is entity-resolved into a unified execution data model.

TED / PPDS Integration ESPD Declarations Arachne Signals API-First GDPR Compliant
Layer 2

Explainable AI Engine

Graph neural networks and explainable AI techniques identify patterns, anomalies, and early-warning signals. Every output includes an evidence chain — traceable to source data, transparent in logic, auditable for institutional review.

XAI / LIME-SHAP Graph Neural Networks Federated Learning Audit Trail EU AI Act Aligned
Layer 3

Intelligence Products

Role-based dashboards for managing authorities, DFIs, programme managers, and analysts. Programme-level risk scores, portfolio comparisons, early-warning alerts, and execution-readiness reports structured for human decision-making.

Risk Scoring Early Warning Portfolio View Exportable Reports Role-Based Access
Capabilities

What Izere does that nothing else does

🔍

Explainable Risk Intelligence

Every risk score comes with a full evidence chain. Izere explains what signals drove the assessment, which data sources were used, and what corrective actions are indicated — suitable for public-sector scrutiny.

  • Evidence-linked risk factors
  • Source-traceable reasoning
  • Contestable and auditable outputs
  • Aligned with EU AI Act transparency requirements
⚡

Early Warning — 2–3 Months Earlier

Traditional monitoring surfaces problems after they become visible. Izere identifies convergent risk signals — coordination failures, capacity constraints, milestone drift — before they translate into delays.

  • Real-time signal monitoring
  • Cross-programme pattern detection
  • Proactive alert feed
  • Configurable risk thresholds
📊

Portfolio-Level Oversight

Compare execution performance across programmes, regions, and investment cycles. Identify structural patterns in delivery failures. Reduce repetitive due diligence by reusing delivery intelligence.

  • Cross-programme benchmarking
  • Absorption rate tracking
  • Partner performance history
  • Reusable delivery intelligence
🏗

Contractor & Subcontractor Intelligence

The most critical — and most invisible — execution risk lies in the subcontractor layer. Izere structures available signals to assess delivery capacity throughout the implementation chain.

  • Delivery track record analysis
  • Capacity signal assessment
  • Coordination risk indicators
  • OLAF-aligned fraud typologies
